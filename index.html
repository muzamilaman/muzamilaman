Home - Is Website ka Maqsad

Is website ka maqsad logon ko Qayamat ki nishaniyon ke baare mein sahi aur mustanad maloomat dena hai, taake hum apni zindagi ko behtar bana sakein aur Allah ki taraf rujoo karein.

Note: Qayamat ka exact waqt sirf Allah Ta'ala ko maloom hai. Hum sirf nishaniyan bayan kar sakte hain.

Qayamat ki Choti Nishaniyan

1. Ilm ka Uth Jana
Deeni ilm kam ho jayega aur jahalat aam ho jayegi.
2. Zina aur Sharab ka Aam Hona
Buraiyan khullam khulla hon gi.
3. Qatal ka Zyada Hona
Logon ko pata nahi hoga ke kyun qatal ho raha hai.
4. Waqt ka Jaldi Guzarna
Saal mahine jaisa, mahina hafte jaisa lagega.
5. Ameer aur Gareeb ka Farq
Log maal jama karne me lage rahen ge.

Qayamat ki Bari Nishaniyan (10 Bari Nishaniyan)

1. Dajjal ka Zahoor
2. Hazrat Isa (A.S) ka Nazool
3. Yajooj Majooj ka Nikalna
4. Dabbat-ul-Arz
5. Sooraj ka Maghrib se Nikalna
6. Dhuan (Dukhan)
7-9. Teen Bade Zalzale - Mashriq, Maghrib aur Jazira-tul-Arab me
10. Aag ka Nikalna jo Logon ko Mahshar ki taraf le jayegi

Quran aur Hadees ki Roshni Mein

Quran: "Log tum se Qayamat ke baare me poochte hain, keh do is ka ilm to sirf Allah ke paas hai." (Surah Al-Ahzab)

Hadees: Nabi ﷺ ne farmaya, Qayamat us waqt tak qaim nahi hogi jab tak 10 bari nishaniyan zahir na ho jayein. (Sahih Muslim)
